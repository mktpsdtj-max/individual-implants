Technical clarity

A Perfect Replacement from Root to Crown

When you're missing a single tooth, outdated treatments like bridges require wearing down your healthy teeth. Our approach is different: the single implant replaces the lost natural root with a small titanium post inserted directly into the jawbone. On top of this new root, we place a custom-made crown.

The new root

A small biocompatible titanium post inserted directly into the jawbone, replacing the lost natural root.

The connector

A pre-fabricated titanium piece that joins the implant to the crown and holds it firmly in place.

Your new tooth

Custom-made to match the color and shape of your other teeth, in porcelain, ceramic or zirconia depending on the package.

Your Implant in 3 Appointments

  1. 1
    radiology Appointment 1

    Diagnosis and Placement

    1. Comprehensive 3D Diagnosis

      Using a state-of-the-art CT scanner, we evaluate the quantity and quality of your bone to ensure the procedure's success.

    2. Implant Placement

      Quickly, under local anesthesia (or sedation options), our specialist places your new artificial root.

  2. 2
    document_scanner Appointment 2

    Preparing Your Crown

    1. Uncovery & Healing Abutment

      If the implant was under the gum, a small incision is made to attach a healing cap that shapes the gum tissue.

    2. 3D Scan or Impression

      A digital scan captures the precise position of the implant, surrounding teeth, and bite.

    3. Shade Matching

      Selection of the exact tooth color to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

  3. 3
    task_alt Appointment 3

    Your Permanent Crown

    The final visit (1–2 weeks later) to place and screw your permanent custom crown.
